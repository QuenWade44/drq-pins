# drq-pins

Weekly fresh Pinterest pins for **School Templates By Dr. Q** on TPT.

- `system/`: the generator (`make_pins.py`), the product catalog, covers and fonts, `STRATEGY.md` (the standing brief) and `RUNBOOK.md` (weekly steps)
- `plans/`: one research plan per week
- `system/rotation.json`: which 20 products sat out each week
- `week-NN/`: that week's 200 pin images and 2 bulk-upload CSVs (A = upload Wednesday, B = upload Saturday; 100 pins each, Pinterest's scheduled-pin limit)
- `data/`: monthly Pinterest and TPT exports that feed the learning loop
